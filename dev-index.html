Our Story

About

Real food. Real people. Real passion.

La Chiquita Tacos Y Más brings the bold, authentic flavours of Latin American street food to Rockford, Illinois.

Every dish on our menu is made fresh from scratch — from the corn and flour tortillas we press by hand each morning, to the house-made salsas and sauces that give our food its signature kick. We source quality ingredients and cook with the same care we'd use for our own families.

Our menu draws from across Latin America: Columbian empanadas, Venezuelan tequenos, Mexican-style breakfast tacos and al pastor, and so much more. Whether you're grabbing a quick morning taco or sitting down with friends for a loaded plate of nachos, we want every bite to feel like the real thing — because it is.

We're proud to serve the Rockford community and be part of its growing food scene. See you soon! 🇲🇽
